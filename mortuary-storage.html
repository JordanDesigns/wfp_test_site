The Casket Jack

A multi-purpose casket lift truck

Solid aluminium throughout, so it stays light enough to move around a prep room and durable enough to live in one. Dual side hand crank wheels with retractable handles let a single operator raise and lower a loaded casket, or two work it together when that suits the room better.

Roller bearing slides keep the mechanism maintenance free, and every fastener is stainless for corrosion resistance.

  • Lift range 12 inches to 34 inches
  • Lift capacity 850 lb
  • Overall weight 145 lb
  • Size 48 inches long by 26 inches wide
  • Mechanism Manual crank lift

Built for the job

Designed around one person working alone

It was designed for transferring oversized remains into oversized caskets and containers, for moving several stacked containers at once, and for loading and unloading vehicles without a second pair of hands. Multiple rubber feet grip any size casket, load rollers keep the slide quiet, and dual side lift levers engage the deck roller when it is time to move the casket off. Tread and swivel brakes lock it in place, and the combination lifting handles double as bumper guards so nothing catches a door frame.

Available options:

  • Digital load scale
  • Modular prep table
  • Short board table top
  • Modular stainless embalming table
  • Lift height to 39 inches, crematorium model
  • Castors from 3 to 8 inches
