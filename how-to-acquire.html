How to Acquire Our Products

We work exclusively with licensed funeral homes to guarantee that every casket, coffin and urn we create is handled with the utmost dignity, professionalism, and care.

For families

Information for Families

If you have your heart set on one of our products for a loved one’s service, you will need to purchase it through a licensed funeral home. We are always happy to coordinate directly with your chosen funeral home to arrange delivery and discuss price.

For funeral service providers

Information for Funeral Service Providers

We would love to partner with you! Please contact our team, and we will happily guide you through the setup process so that we can become a new supplier and resource for you.

Get in touch

Talk to us about your next order

Whether you are a funeral home ready to set up an account, or a family asking your funeral director about one of our products, we are glad to help. Send us a note or call the office.
