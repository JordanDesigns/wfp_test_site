Our story

A dedicated partner to funeral homes

For over two decades, Williams Funeral Products has stood as a dedicated partner to funeral homes, stepping in with exceptional service and seamless solutions exactly when you need them.

True service means anticipating the evolving needs of the families you care for, which is why we place earth-friendly innovation at the heart of everything we make. Every casket is crafted right here in Canada using 100% Canadian materials, bringing true affordability to sustainable, green-burial options.

By eliminating unnecessary environmental impact without compromising on premium craftsmanship, we ensure your funeral home can confidently offer authentic, earth-friendly solutions. When unexpected challenges arise or families look for mindful, sustainable choices, we are here with the practical, local support your business can rely on.

What we stand for

01

Proudly Canadian

Canadian materials, worked by skilled local artisans. Every casket we make is built here, which keeps quality within arm’s reach and supports the communities we work in.

02

Environmentally Friendly

We prioritise earth-friendly materials throughout production. Green burial should not be a premium product, so we have built affordability into sustainability rather than treating it as an upgrade.

03

Responsive Service

We understand the urgency of your needs and are committed to providing swift, dependable service when it matters most, because the families in your care cannot wait.

Where we work

Local delivery, national shipping

Our caskets and repair services are offered to funeral homes in Southern Ontario and to businesses across Nova Scotia. Everyday essentials such as headblocks, mailers, pouches and body bags can be ordered from anywhere in Canada.
