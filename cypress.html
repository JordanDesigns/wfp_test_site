The Cypress

More than an advancement in green burial design

The Cypress is a profound testament to the natural cycle of life, purpose-built to help families return to the earth with pure intention. Crafted right here in Canada from 100% locally sourced materials, this innovative, completely metal-free casket is engineered to achieve perfect harmony with the earth’s natural resonance, offering a dignified, affordable path back to the soil.

The name

Why we call it the Cypress

We named this design The Cypress because of the ancient, powerful symbolism held within its namesake. Throughout history, the cypress tree has stood as a universal symbol of the soul’s spiritual journey, representing mourning, remembrance, and the transition between life and eternity.

Known for its soaring upward growth, the cypress bridges the gap between the earth and the heavens, embodying a timeless strength that provides comfort in moments of grief. Because the living tree is an evergreen, it symbolises the enduring nature of memory, reminding us that while the physical form returns to the soil, a person’s legacy remains forever alive.

For your funeral home

Honouring the person and the planet

When a funeral home chooses The Cypress, they are providing a solution that honours both the person who has passed and the planet they leave behind. Its thoughtful, lightweight construction ensures seamless, reliable handling for directors, while its entirely organic, sustainable design allows the body to gently dissolve back into the earth, completing the circle of life exactly as nature intended.

  • Construction 100% metal free
  • Materials 100% Canadian, locally sourced
  • Handling Lightweight construction
  • Suited to Green burial
