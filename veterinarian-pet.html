The pet container

Made for the animals you actually see

A pressed board shell with a lift off lid and a recessed handle at each end, made in more than one size so a practice can hold stock for a range of animals rather than ordering per case.

Tell us the sizes you go through most and we will quote you on a standing order.

The lid

A heart, and a set of paw prints

The lid is engraved rather than printed, so the motif is cut into the board and will not lift or fade. A heart outline, with paw prints tracking across it and away.

It is a small thing, but it is the part the family sees, and it is the difference between a box and something they are willing to carry out to the car.
